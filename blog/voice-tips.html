Studio Tip

Use Proper Mic Distance

Keep 6–8 inches distance from microphone for balanced sound capture.

Warm Up Before Recording

Before starting any professional voice recording, warming up your voice helps improve tone, reduce strain, and increase clarity. Simple breathing exercises and vowel practice can prepare your vocal cords.

🎙 Quick Voice Tip

Drink warm water before recording to reduce throat dryness and improve vocal smoothness.

Maintain Correct Microphone Distance

Keeping proper distance from the microphone helps avoid distortion, breathing noise, and unwanted popping sounds.

"Professional voice artists focus more on consistency than loudness."

Control Breathing Naturally

Controlled breathing helps maintain rhythm and avoids unnecessary pauses during long voice sessions.
